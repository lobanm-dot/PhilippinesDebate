Dossier 03 · Philippines

Before the Debate: Read the Image

This 1899 cartoon came out while Americans argued about overseas expansion. It is useful evidence, but it also uses racist stereotypes and treats colonized people like children.

03
LOUIS DALRYMPLE · PUCK · JAN. 25, 1899

What problem does this cartoon point out?

Uncle Sam is the teacher. Cuba, Puerto Rico, Hawaii, and the Philippines are shown as children who must be taught how to govern.

CLUE: Americans said government should depend on the people’s consent. But the cartoon shows Uncle Sam ruling new territories whether they agree or not.

What actually happened?

The Senate approved the Treaty of Paris on February 6, 1899. The Philippines came under U.S. rule. Fighting between U.S. and Filipino forces had already begun on February 4. Americans kept arguing: Did expansion make the U.S. stronger, or did it go against American ideas about self-government?
